腕腕心情隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日 · 开发者：曹继龙（个人开发者）

腕腕心情是一个在 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上查看身体状态的 App。我们的原则是：健康数值只在你的设备上计算。我们没有自己的服务器，不收集、不出售健康数据，也读不到你的 iCloud。只有你自己打开好友并勾选之后，几句文字状态才会放进你的 iCloud，分享给这位好友。

1. 我们读取哪些数据

在你同意后，腕腕心情通过 Apple「健康」（HealthKit）读取以下数据。其中健康数据和运动路线属于敏感个人信息，只在你的设备上处理：

  • 睡眠（包括睡眠阶段和午睡）、睡眠时手腕温度、睡眠时呼吸紊乱；
  • 心率、静息心率、心率变异性（HRV，包括 SDNN 和 RMSSD）、一分钟心率恢复、呼吸频率、血氧、最大摄氧量；
  • 血压（如果你用血压计同步到了「健康」）；
  • 步数、步行跑步距离、骑车距离、游泳距离、推轮椅距离、爬楼层数、活动能量、锻炼和站立时间、活动圆环、户外日光时间；
  • 步行速度、步长、步行不对称性、双支撑时间、步行稳定性、上下楼梯速度；
  • 体能训练记录，包括运动路线（位置轨迹）、骑行功率和踏频；
  • 体重、体脂率、出生日期和生理性别（用于计算个人范围、心率区间和身体年龄；出生日期还用于生日当天的小惊喜，以及在星空里标出你的星座）；
  • 正念时长、你在「健康」里记录的心情；
  • 月经和排卵试纸记录（如果你有）。

这些数据用于计算今天的状态、恢复、实时压力、睡眠、精力预算、身体年龄、长期趋势和报告，并显示在 App、小组件和 Apple Watch 上。

你可以只开放其中一部分，也可以随时在 iPhone「设置」→「健康」→「数据访问与设备」→「腕腕心情」里修改。没有开放的项目，对应的卡片会空着。

2. 我们写入哪些数据

只有你在腕腕心情里主动记录经期时，才会把这条记录写入「健康」。除此之外，腕腕心情不会向「健康」写入任何数据。写入的经期记录可以在「健康」App 里查看和删除。

3. 数据存在哪里

  • 所有计算都在你的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上完成；
  • 计算结果和你的设置保存在设备本地，供 App、小组件和表盘显示。iPhone 和 Apple Watch 之间通过系统的 WatchConnectivity 直接传输；
  • 睡眠时长、心率、HRV、步数、运动记录这些健康数值不会发送到我们这里（我们没有服务器），也不会上传；
  • 如果你开启了 iPhone 的 iCloud 云备份，系统备份可能包含腕腕心情保存在本机的分析结果。这部分由 Apple 按 iCloud 备份的方式加密保存，我们无法访问；
  • 删除腕腕心情会删除它在设备上保存的数据。「健康」里的原始数据不受影响。已经放进 iCloud 的好友内容，请先在 App 里删除好友；仍留在 iCloud 里的，可在系统「设置」→ Apple 账户 → iCloud 里管理。

4. 好友

好友默认关闭，什么都不分享。你用邀请码加一位好友之后，可以逐项打开。对方只能看到你打开的那几句，存在你和对方的 iCloud（CloudKit）里，不经过我们的服务器，我们也读不到。

可能分享的内容只有这些文字，不含数值、时间和运动名称：

  • 心情表情，以及它是几点更新的；
  • 恢复：很好 / 还行 / 偏低；
  • 昨晚睡够了还是没睡够（按你自己的睡眠目标）；
  • 今天的步数目标走够没有、三个活动圆环是否都合上、今天有没有运动过；
  • 你允许被催运动时，是否已经到了可以催的时候；
  • 一起进行的小挑战有没有达标；
  • 你和好友之间的招呼：戳一戳、早安、晚安、加油、想你了。

你可以把一位好友设为贴心人（只能设一位），TA 会多收到这些：你累了的时候的一句话（比如「昨晚没怎么睡够」，可以在贴心人设置里关掉）；最近 14 天里你拿到太阳的是哪几天；你给小团子起的名字和它正在穿的配饰。你们互相设为贴心人以后，对方的小团子会出现在你的小屋里。这些同样只是文字，不含数值。

不会分享：心率、HRV、睡眠时长、入睡和起床时间、步数、圆环进度、运动类型和时长、经期、体重、位置和运动路线。

没起名字时，好友看到的是你的 iCloud 名字。你起的名字最多 12 个字。邀请码存在 CloudKit 的公共数据库里，只包含邀请码、过期时间和一条分享链接，24 小时内有效、只能用一次，不含健康内容。关掉某一项或删除好友后，下次同步对方就看不到了。删除好友会删掉这段共享。

好友更新时，iCloud 会给你的手机发一条没有横幅的静默推送，把 App 叫醒去取新内容。提醒文字由你的设备本地生成。

5. 你主动分享的内容

导出 CSV 数据报告、就诊一页纸、分享图片或发送反馈邮件，都由你自己发起，并通过系统的分享面板或邮件 App 发出。接收方由你选择；只有你发给我们的邮件，我们才会收到。请在分享前确认内容，健康数据一旦发出，就由接收方处理。

6. 地图

查看带路线的运动时，App 使用 Apple 地图显示轨迹，系统会向 Apple 请求对应区域的地图。这一过程适用 Apple 的隐私政策，我们拿不到这些请求。运动路线不会分享给好友。

7. 我们不做的事

  • 不使用任何第三方统计、广告、崩溃收集或追踪 SDK；
  • 不跨 App 或网站追踪你；
  • 不把健康数据用于广告、营销或数据挖掘，也不出售。除了你主动勾选、经 iCloud 分享给好友的那些文字状态，我们不把健康数据提供给任何人；
  • 不要求注册账号。好友使用你已经登录的 iCloud，不用另外注册。

8. 会员订阅

会员为自动续期的年订阅，通过 Apple 的 App Store 购买和续订。我们只从系统获得是否有效、到期时间这类购买状态，拿不到你的姓名、Apple ID 或付款信息。管理或取消订阅，请前往 iPhone「设置」→ 你的名字 →「订阅」。

9. 通知

身体信号提醒和每日简报默认关闭。打开后，通知由设备本地生成，内容不会经过我们。好友提醒也是设备在取到 iCloud 里的新内容之后本地生成的。你可以随时在 App 设置或 iPhone「设置」→「通知」里关闭。

10. 你的权利

  • 查看：腕腕心情使用的数据和计算结果都可以在 App 里直接看到，也可以导出 CSV。好友能看到的内容在分享设置里逐项写明；
  • 撤回授权：在「健康」的数据访问设置里随时关闭任何一项。好友分享可以逐项关掉，也可以删除好友；
  • 删除：删除 App 即删除本地数据；写入「健康」的经期记录可在「健康」App 里删除。删除好友会删掉这段 iCloud 共享；
  • 咨询与投诉：通过下方邮箱联系我们。好友的名字或消息不合适，也可以用这个邮箱举报。

11. 医疗声明

腕腕心情不是医疗器械，不提供诊断或治疗。所有状态、分数、身体年龄和建议都是基于你的数据的估算，仅供日常参考。好友看到的文字状态同样不是诊断。如有身体不适，请咨询医生。

12. 未成年人

腕腕心情不面向 14 周岁以下的儿童。未满 18 周岁的用户，请在监护人同意下使用。

13. 政策变更

如果本政策有实质变化，我们会更新本页的生效日期。本次变更说明了可选的好友共享：文字状态存在你自己的 iCloud 里，以及贴心人会多收到的内容；另外新增读取骑车、游泳和推轮椅的距离，只在本机用来点亮星空；出生日期也会在本机用来找出你的星座，不做运势。

14. 联系我们

有任何问题，请发邮件到 358136684@qq.com，我们会在 15 个工作日内回复。

Wrist Mood Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Developer: Jilong Cao (individual developer)

Wrist Mood is an app for understanding your body on iPhone and Apple Watch. Our principle is simple: health numbers are processed only on your devices. We run no servers of our own, we do not sell health data, and we cannot read your iCloud. Only after you turn on Friends and choose what to share does a short written status go into your iCloud for that friend.

1. Data we read

With your permission, XWatch reads the following from Apple Health (HealthKit). Health data and workout routes are sensitive, and they are processed only on your devices:

  • Sleep (including sleep stages and naps), sleeping wrist temperature and sleeping breathing disturbances;
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV, both SDNN and RMSSD), one-minute heart rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen and VO₂ max;
  • Blood pressure, if your monitor syncs it to Health;
  • Steps, walking and running distance, cycling, swimming and wheelchair distance, flights climbed, active energy, exercise and stand time, Activity rings and time in daylight;
  • Walking speed, step length, walking asymmetry, double support time, walking steadiness, and stair ascent and descent speed;
  • Workouts, including routes (location tracks), cycling power and cadence;
  • Weight, body fat percentage, date of birth and biological sex, used for personal ranges, heart rate zones and body age; date of birth is also used for a small birthday surprise and to mark your star sign in the star sky;
  • Mindful minutes and the moods you log in Health;
  • Menstrual flow and ovulation test results, if any.

XWatch uses this data to calculate today's status, recovery, live stress, sleep, energy budget, body age, long-term trends and reports, and shows the results in the app, in widgets and on Apple Watch.

You can allow only some of these types, and change your choice at any time in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → XWatch. Cards for types you haven't allowed stay empty.

2. Data we write

XWatch writes to Health only when you log a period in the app. It writes nothing else. You can view and delete those entries in the Health app.

3. Where data is stored

  • All calculations run on your iPhone and Apple Watch;
  • Results and your settings are stored locally on the device for the app, widgets and watch complications. iPhone and Apple Watch exchange them directly through the system's WatchConnectivity;
  • Health numbers such as sleep duration, heart rate, HRV, step counts and workout records are never sent to us (we have no servers) and are not uploaded;
  • If iCloud Backup is turned on for your iPhone, the system backup may include the analysis results Wrist Mood keeps on the device. Apple encrypts these as part of iCloud Backup, and we cannot access them;
  • Deleting Wrist Mood removes the data it stores on the device. Your original data in Health is not affected. Delete a friend in the app first if you want that shared iCloud data removed. Anything still in iCloud can be managed in Settings → your Apple Account → iCloud.

4. Friends

Friends is off until you turn items on. After you add someone with an invite code, you choose each item. That person sees only the lines you enabled. They are stored in your iCloud and theirs (CloudKit). They do not pass through a server of ours, and we cannot read them.

What can be shared is words only, never a number, a clock time or a workout name:

  • A mood face, and when it was updated;
  • Recovery as doing well, okay, or low;
  • Whether last night met your own sleep goal;
  • Whether today's step goal was met, whether all three Activity rings closed, and whether you worked out today;
  • If you allow nudges, whether it is time for one;
  • Whether a shared challenge target was reached;
  • Notes between you: a poke, good morning, good night, a cheer, or "miss you".

You can make one friend your special friend. That person also receives: a short line when you are worn out (for example "didn't sleep much last night", which you can turn off in the special friend settings); which of the last 14 days earned you a sun; and your mascot's name and the accessory it is wearing. Once you have made each other special friends, their mascot appears in your mascot's house. These are words as well, never numbers.

Never shared: heart rate, HRV, sleep duration, bed and wake times, step counts, ring progress, workout type or duration, menstrual data, weight, location or workout routes.

If you have not chosen a name, friends see your iCloud name. A name you set is at most 12 characters. An invite code is stored in CloudKit's public database and contains only the code, an expiry time and a share link. It lasts 24 hours and can be used once. It contains no health content. Turning an item off or deleting the friend removes it on the next sync. Deleting a friend deletes that share.

When a friend writes something, iCloud sends your iPhone a silent push with no banner, to wake the app and fetch the update. The alert text is then created on your device.

5. What you choose to share

Exporting a CSV report or a clinic summary, sharing an image, or sending a feedback email is always started by you, through the system share sheet or your mail app. You choose the recipient, and we only receive emails you send to us. Please review the content before you share it; once health data is sent, the recipient handles it.

6. Maps

When you view a workout with a route, the app shows it on Apple Maps, and the system requests map data for that area from Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to those requests, and we have no access to them. Workout routes are not shared with friends.

7. What we don't do

  • We use no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking SDKs;
  • We don't track you across apps or websites;
  • We don't use health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we never sell it. Aside from the written status you choose to share with a friend through your iCloud, we don't provide health data to anyone;
  • We don't require an account. Friends uses the iCloud account you are already signed into.

8. Subscription

Membership is an auto-renewable annual subscription, purchased and renewed through Apple's App Store. We receive only purchase status, such as whether it is active and when it expires. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. To manage or cancel, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

9. Notifications

Body signal reminders and daily briefs are off by default. When you turn them on, notifications are created locally on your device and never pass through us. Friend alerts are also created on the device after it fetches new iCloud content. You can turn them off at any time in the app's settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

10. Your rights

  • Access: everything Wrist Mood uses and calculates is shown in the app and can be exported as CSV. What a friend can see is listed next to each sharing switch;
  • Withdraw permission: turn off any Health data type at any time in Health's data access settings. Turn off friend sharing item by item, or delete the friend;
  • Delete: deleting the app removes its local data; period entries written to Health can be deleted in the Health app. Deleting a friend deletes that iCloud share;
  • Questions and complaints: contact us at the email below. You can use the same address to report a friend's name or message.

11. Medical disclaimer

Wrist Mood is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. All statuses, scores, body age and suggestions are estimates based on your data, for everyday reference only. A written status a friend sees is not a diagnosis either. If you feel unwell, please consult a doctor.

12. Children

Wrist Mood is not intended for children under 14. Users under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's consent.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we update the effective date on this page. This update describes optional friend sharing: a written status stored in your own iCloud, and what a special friend also receives. It also adds reading cycling, swimming and wheelchair distance, used on your device only to light up the star sky, and uses your date of birth on your device to find your star sign, with no horoscopes.

14. Contact

For any questions, email 358136684@qq.com. We reply within 15 working days.
