小棉袄隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日 · 开发者：曹继龙（个人开发者）

小棉袄是一个在 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上查看身体状态的 App。我们的原则是：你的健康数据只在你的设备上计算。我们没有自己的服务器，不收集、不上传、不出售任何健康数据，也不收集能识别你身份的信息。

1. 我们读取哪些数据

在你同意后，小棉袄通过 Apple「健康」（HealthKit）读取以下数据。其中健康数据和运动路线属于敏感个人信息，只在你的设备上处理：

  • 睡眠（包括睡眠阶段和午睡）、睡眠时手腕温度、睡眠时呼吸紊乱；
  • 心率、静息心率、心率变异性（HRV，包括 SDNN 和 RMSSD）、一分钟心率恢复、呼吸频率、血氧、最大摄氧量；
  • 血压（如果你用血压计同步到了「健康」）；
  • 步数、步行跑步距离、爬楼层数、活动能量、锻炼和站立时间、活动圆环、户外日光时间；
  • 步行速度、步长、步行不对称性、双支撑时间、步行稳定性、上下楼梯速度；
  • 体能训练记录，包括运动路线（位置轨迹）、骑行功率和踏频；
  • 体重、体脂率、出生日期和生理性别（用于计算个人范围、心率区间和身体年龄）；
  • 正念时长、你在「健康」里记录的心情；
  • 月经和排卵试纸记录（如果你有）。

这些数据用于计算今天的状态、恢复、实时压力、睡眠、精力预算、身体年龄、长期趋势和报告，并显示在 App、小组件和 Apple Watch 上。

你可以只开放其中一部分，也可以随时在 iPhone「设置」→「健康」→「数据访问与设备」→「小棉袄」里修改。没有开放的项目，对应的卡片会空着。

2. 我们写入哪些数据

只有你在小棉袄里主动记录经期时，才会把这条记录写入「健康」。除此之外，小棉袄不会向「健康」写入任何数据。写入的经期记录可以在「健康」App 里查看和删除。

3. 数据存在哪里

  • 所有计算都在你的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上完成；
  • 计算结果和你的设置保存在设备本地，供 App、小组件和表盘显示。iPhone 和 Apple Watch 之间通过系统的 WatchConnectivity 直接传输；
  • 健康数据不会发送到我们这里（我们没有服务器），也不会上传到 iCloud 云盘或 CloudKit；
  • 如果你开启了 iPhone 的 iCloud 云备份，系统备份可能包含小棉袄保存在本机的分析结果。这部分由 Apple 按 iCloud 备份的方式加密保存，我们无法访问；
  • 删除小棉袄会删除它在设备上保存的数据。「健康」里的原始数据不受影响。

4. 你主动分享的内容

导出 CSV 数据报告、就诊一页纸、分享图片或发送反馈邮件，都由你自己发起，并通过系统的分享面板或邮件 App 发出。接收方由你选择；只有你发给我们的邮件，我们才会收到。请在分享前确认内容，健康数据一旦发出，就由接收方处理。

5. 地图

查看带路线的运动时，App 使用 Apple 地图显示轨迹，系统会向 Apple 请求对应区域的地图。这一过程适用 Apple 的隐私政策，我们拿不到这些请求。

6. 我们不做的事

  • 不使用任何第三方统计、广告、崩溃收集或追踪 SDK；
  • 不跨 App 或网站追踪你；
  • 不把健康数据用于广告、营销或数据挖掘，也不出售或提供给任何人；
  • 不要求注册账号。

7. 会员订阅

会员为自动续期的年订阅，通过 Apple 的 App Store 购买和续订。我们只从系统获得是否有效、到期时间这类购买状态，拿不到你的姓名、Apple ID 或付款信息。管理或取消订阅，请前往 iPhone「设置」→ 你的名字 →「订阅」。

8. 通知

身体信号提醒和每日简报默认关闭。打开后，通知由设备本地生成，内容不会经过我们。你可以随时在 App 设置或 iPhone「设置」→「通知」里关闭。

9. 你的权利

  • 查看：小棉袄使用的数据和计算结果都可以在 App 里直接看到，也可以导出 CSV；
  • 撤回授权：在「健康」的数据访问设置里随时关闭任何一项；
  • 删除：删除 App 即删除本地数据；写入「健康」的经期记录可在「健康」App 里删除；
  • 咨询与投诉：通过下方邮箱联系我们。

10. 医疗声明

小棉袄不是医疗器械，不提供诊断或治疗。所有状态、分数、身体年龄和建议都是基于你的数据的估算，仅供日常参考。如有身体不适，请咨询医生。

11. 未成年人

小棉袄不面向 14 周岁以下的儿童。未满 18 周岁的用户，请在监护人同意下使用。

12. 政策变更

如果本政策有实质变化，例如新增需要联网的功能，我们会在 App 内提示，并更新本页的生效日期。

13. 联系我们

有任何问题，请发邮件到 358136684@qq.com，我们会在 15 个工作日内回复。

XWatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Developer: Jilong Cao (individual developer)

XWatch (小棉袄) is an app for understanding your body on iPhone and Apple Watch. Our principle is simple: your health data is processed only on your devices. We run no servers of our own, and we do not collect, upload or sell any health data or any information that identifies you.

1. Data we read

With your permission, XWatch reads the following from Apple Health (HealthKit). Health data and workout routes are sensitive, and they are processed only on your devices:

  • Sleep (including sleep stages and naps), sleeping wrist temperature and sleeping breathing disturbances;
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV, both SDNN and RMSSD), one-minute heart rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen and VO₂ max;
  • Blood pressure, if your monitor syncs it to Health;
  • Steps, walking and running distance, flights climbed, active energy, exercise and stand time, Activity rings and time in daylight;
  • Walking speed, step length, walking asymmetry, double support time, walking steadiness, and stair ascent and descent speed;
  • Workouts, including routes (location tracks), cycling power and cadence;
  • Weight, body fat percentage, date of birth and biological sex, used for personal ranges, heart rate zones and body age;
  • Mindful minutes and the moods you log in Health;
  • Menstrual flow and ovulation test results, if any.

XWatch uses this data to calculate today's status, recovery, live stress, sleep, energy budget, body age, long-term trends and reports, and shows the results in the app, in widgets and on Apple Watch.

You can allow only some of these types, and change your choice at any time in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → XWatch. Cards for types you haven't allowed stay empty.

2. Data we write

XWatch writes to Health only when you log a period in the app. It writes nothing else. You can view and delete those entries in the Health app.

3. Where data is stored

  • All calculations run on your iPhone and Apple Watch;
  • Results and your settings are stored locally on the device for the app, widgets and watch complications. iPhone and Apple Watch exchange them directly through the system's WatchConnectivity;
  • Health data is never sent to us (we have no servers) and is not uploaded to iCloud Drive or CloudKit;
  • If iCloud Backup is turned on for your iPhone, the system backup may include the analysis results XWatch keeps on the device. Apple encrypts these as part of iCloud Backup, and we cannot access them;
  • Deleting XWatch removes the data it stores on the device. Your original data in Health is not affected.

4. What you choose to share

Exporting a CSV report or a clinic summary, sharing an image, or sending a feedback email is always started by you, through the system share sheet or your mail app. You choose the recipient, and we only receive emails you send to us. Please review the content before you share it; once health data is sent, the recipient handles it.

5. Maps

When you view a workout with a route, the app shows it on Apple Maps, and the system requests map data for that area from Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to those requests, and we have no access to them.

6. What we don't do

  • We use no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking SDKs;
  • We don't track you across apps or websites;
  • We don't use health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we never sell or provide it to anyone;
  • We don't require an account.

7. Subscription

Membership is an auto-renewable annual subscription, purchased and renewed through Apple's App Store. We receive only purchase status, such as whether it is active and when it expires. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. To manage or cancel, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

8. Notifications

Body signal reminders and daily briefs are off by default. When you turn them on, notifications are created locally on your device and never pass through us. You can turn them off at any time in the app's settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

9. Your rights

  • Access: everything XWatch uses and calculates is shown in the app and can be exported as CSV;
  • Withdraw permission: turn off any data type at any time in Health's data access settings;
  • Delete: deleting the app removes its local data; period entries written to Health can be deleted in the Health app;
  • Questions and complaints: contact us at the email below.

10. Medical disclaimer

XWatch is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. All statuses, scores, body age and suggestions are estimates based on your data, for everyday reference only. If you feel unwell, please consult a doctor.

11. Children

XWatch is not intended for children under 14. Users under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's consent.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, for example if we add a feature that needs a network service, we will let you know in the app and update the effective date on this page.

13. Contact

For any questions, email 358136684@qq.com. We reply within 15 working days.
